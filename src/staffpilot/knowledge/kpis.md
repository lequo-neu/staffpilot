# Metric definitions

## Net value

Expected uplift value earned in the planning year minus salary cost and firing cost in the same year.

## Uplift value

Sum over assigned advertisers of the expected uplift (13.5 percent) times the projected annual budget, counted for advertisers who sign up in the planning year, including those whose support starts in the following January.

## Salary cost

Annual salary divided by twelve, times agents on payroll each month. Payroll includes active agents and agents in ramp up.

## Firing cost

40 percent of annual salary for each notice given.

## Service rate

Share of eligible advertisers who sign up in the planning year and are assigned an agent, at any time, before their 60 day wait runs out.

## Wait time

Days between sign up and assignment for advertisers who were assigned. Reported as the mean and the 90th percentile.

## Utilization

Occupied slot days divided by available slot days, where available slots are ten per active agent.

## Idle agents

Unused slots divided by ten, averaged over the days of the month.

## ROI

Uplift value divided by total cost.
